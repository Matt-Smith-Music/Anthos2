Input

Plug the guitar into an audio interface and listen on headphones plugged into the interface: the phone or laptop's own mic and speakers will feed back. Set the interface gain so hard strumming just lights the amber LEDs on the In meter.

Captures

Captures are profiles of real amps and pedals, free on TONE3000. Pick the .zip it downloads as it is. Both NAM A2 (the current format, with Full and Lite in one file) and the older A1 captures play. Lite is the one for phones; Full needs a reasonably quick computer. If the sound crackles, switch to Lite or pick a lighter capture. Bass, Middle, Treble and Presence still work as an EQ after a capture; its gain is fixed by the capture itself.
