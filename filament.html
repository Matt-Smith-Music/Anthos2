Honest limits

Tracking works. Peak picking with parabolic interpolation plus phase-derived frequency puts each partial within a few cents. You can read vibrato straight off the plot.

Grouping is crude. Two notes an octave apart with the same onset and no vibrato are genuinely indistinguishable from one note. This app will merge them and say so. Melodyne has the same failure and hands you Note Assignment; here, resynthesise and listen.

Distortion defeats it. Intermodulation creates partials belonging to no note.

Drums and full mixes. Cymbals, snares and reverb are noise, and noise has no partials to track — you get thousands of short fragments instead. Grouping those into notes is meaningless. Use a box and Hear the audio in it for that material: it filters the original recording rather than rebuilding it, so noise and transients survive intact.
